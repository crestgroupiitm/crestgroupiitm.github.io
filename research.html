01 Granted

Rotary Kiln Incinerator for Generating Energy from Municipal Solid Waste

Vinu R (Chemical Engineering); Chakravarthy S R (Aerospace Engineering); Prasad B V S S S (Mechanical Engineering); Sundararajan (Mechanical Engineering)

  • IN 517599
  • PCT WO2022029802
  • IITM IDF Ref. 2026

India · TRL 4, technology validated in lab · Available for licensing

An improved rotary kiln that sustains combustion of unsegregated municipal solid waste while recovering heat energy and treating emissions. The design addresses the low calorific value and high moisture content that make conventional MSW incineration difficult, using an internal rotating sieve mesh for ash removal, a shell-and-tube heat exchanger for heat recovery, and a filter plus wet scrubber for emission control.

02 Granted

A Process for Manufacture of Hydrogenated Hydroxyl Terminated Polybutadiene (HHTPB) using Pd–Zr–Activated Charcoal as Catalyst

Ravi Krishnan Vinu; Abhijit P Deshpande (Dept. of Chemical Engineering)

  • IN 537079
  • IITM IDF Ref. 2690

India · Advanced materials industry · Available for licensing

A process for the selective hydrogenation of the unsaturated double bonds of hydroxyl terminated polybutadiene, achieving degrees of hydrogenation from 5–90%. The reaction is carried out in the presence of hydrogen/nitrogen gas, a Pd–Zr/charcoal catalyst, and an organic solvent system at 180–270 °C. A principal application is as a binder for soft explosives.

03 Granted

Method of Detecting the Extent of Oil Degradation

Basu, Amiyo K.; Vinu, R.; Tripathi, Anand Kumar · Applicant: Caterpillar Inc.

  • AU 2015283997 B2
  • PCT WO 2016/004270
  • PCT/US2015/038932

Australia · Standard patent accepted 19 December 2019

A method of detecting the extent of degradation of an oil by analysing samples when fresh and when used, calculating electrochemical property data for each, determining whether that data correlates with degradation marker data, and using the electrochemical property to detect the extent of degradation. Applicable to mineral, synthetic, and semi-synthetic oils, the method maximises oil usage by monitoring quality over time and identifying degradation trends.

Mission & vision

X2Fuels and Energy is a Chennai-based deep technology startup incubated at the IIT Madras ecosystem, positioned at the intersection of waste management, renewable energy, and circular economy. Its mission is to address the global solid waste crisis by transforming diverse waste streams into valuable fuels and chemicals, reducing landfill methane emissions and environmental pollution.

Prof. Vinu Ravikrishnan serves as Co-Founder and Director, bringing the laboratory’s academic and research expertise directly into industrial deployment.

Core technology: hydrothermal liquefaction

The central process is hydrothermal liquefaction (HTL), a thermochemical conversion that mimics natural fossil fuel formation, compressing it from millions of years into hours under high-temperature and high-pressure conditions.

  • Converts wet biomass and mixed waste without energy-intensive drying
  • Handles MSW, agricultural residues, plastics, sewage sludge, and algae
  • Produces bio-crude with composition similar to petroleum crude
  • Compatible with existing refinery infrastructure
  • Co-processes plastics with biomass to enhance fuel quality

Scale-up milestones

  1. 2018

    Incubated at IIT Madras

    X2Fuels and Energy founded within the IIT Madras ecosystem, with Prof. Vinu Ravikrishnan as Co-Founder and Director.

  2. Lab scale

    100 L batch HTL reactor

    The first scale-up step out of bench chemistry, establishing HTL operation on real waste feedstocks.

  3. 2024

    30 L/hr continuous HTL unit

    The world’s first continuous indigenous hydrothermal liquefaction system for unsegregated municipal solid waste, reported in Applied Energy.

  4. Demonstration

    ~5 tonnes-per-day continuous plant

    A commercial demonstration plant, one of the first of its kind in India, converting industrial and municipal waste into crude-like oil and char in continuous operation.

  5. Industry

    Partnership & equity backing

    Engineers India Limited as industrial partner and Tube Investments of India as equity partner, with R&D infrastructure from NCCRD.

Scale-up journey

From a 100-litre batch HTL reactor, the company progressed to a continuous commercial demonstration plant of ~5 tonnes per day, one of the first of its kind in India, converting industrial and municipal waste into crude-like oil and char in a continuous operational mode.
