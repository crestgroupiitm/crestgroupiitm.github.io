Laboratory Mission

Pioneering sustainable horizons in reaction engineering

We decode the chemistry that turns society's most complex residues, from biomass and plastics to municipal wastes, into the fuels, chemicals, and materials of a circular economy.

The laboratory identifies and analyzes the chemical transformations driving the thermochemical and catalytic conversion of diverse feedstocks, including lignocellulosic biomass, waste plastics, microalgae, coals, and municipal solid wastes, into high-value chemicals, fuel molecules, and bioproducts.

We systematically unravel the reaction pathways and kinetics underlying catalytic fast pyrolysis, hydropyrolysis, microwave-assisted pyrolysis, and hydrodeoxygenation, and advance the process intensification and scale-up of hydrothermal liquefaction toward commercial deployment.

In parallel, the laboratory investigates the thermal and electrical ageing of lubricating oils and polymer composites, correlating predictive ageing markers with the structural and physicochemical properties of the materials.

Research Framework

Five Pillars of Inquiry

Our research portfolio connects feedstock transformation, catalyst design, and scalable process engineering. Each pillar is a distinct entry point into sustainable thermochemical innovation, from fundamental chemistry to pilot-scale deployment.

01

Chemical Transformations in Feedstock Conversion

Fundamental chemistry underlying the thermochemical and catalytic conversion of diverse organic feedstocks, including lignocellulosic biomass, waste plastics, microalgae, coals, and municipal solid waste, into valuable chemicals, fuel molecules, and bioproducts.

Feedstock ConversionCatalysis
 02

Reaction Pathways & Kinetics of Thermal Processes

Unraveling the intricate reaction networks and kinetics underlying fast pyrolysis, hydropyrolysis, microwave-assisted pyrolysis, and hydrodeoxygenation of complex feedstocks.

Reaction KineticsPyrolysis
 03

Process Intensification & Scale-up of Hydrothermal Liquefaction

Bridging lab science to industrial application by generating bio-crude and valuable chemicals from municipal solid waste, plastics, and biomass residues through continuous hydrothermal processing.

Hydrothermal LiquefactionScale-up
 04

Thermal & Electrical Ageing of Materials

Investigating degradation mechanisms of lubricating oils and polymer composites under operational stress, correlating ageing markers with structural and physicochemical properties for predictive lifetime assessment.

Materials AgeingDielectrics
 05

Advanced Functional Materials for Environmental Decontamination

Designing novel catalysts and functional materials for photocatalysis, sonophotocatalysis, and high-capacity adsorption to degrade recalcitrant pollutants and emerging contaminants.
