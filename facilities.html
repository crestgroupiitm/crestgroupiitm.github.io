Facility catalogue

Laboratory infrastructure.

The CREST Laboratory combines custom-built thermochemical reactor platforms with analytical and synthesis infrastructure that supports feedstock conversion, catalyst development, and product characterization. Each instrument is integral to the lab's research in thermochemical conversion, catalyst characterisation, and bio-product analysis.

Reactors & Conversion Systems

3
Reactor System

High Pressure Stirred Reactor

Autoclave-type batch reactor for high-pressure catalytic conversion, hydrothermal liquefaction, and solvent-phase upgrading experiments at controlled temperature and pressure.

Reactor System

High Pressure Stirred Reactor for Hydro-deoxygenation

Dedicated high-pressure system for hydro-deoxygenation (HDO) of bio-oil model compounds and real bio-oils under hydrogen atmosphere and elevated pressure.

Inert Atmosphere

Inert Atmosphere Glove Box

Provides a moisture- and oxygen-free working environment for air-sensitive catalyst synthesis, sample preparation, and handling of reactive intermediates.

Chromatography & Mass Spectrometry

6
Py-GC/MS

Shimadzu GCMS QP2020 + JHI-07 Hybrid Pyrolyzer

Integrated Py-GC/MS platform with a double-shot hybrid pyrolyzer enabling flash pyrolysis at up to 2000 °C/s for evolved gas identification and quantification.

Shimadzu
Py-GC/MS

Pyrolysis GC/MS with Auto Shot Sampler

Automated multi-position sample carousel enables high-throughput Py-GC/MS screening of biomass and polymer feedstocks with reproducible pyrolysis conditions.

Py-GC/MS

PY-2D-GC/MS (Comprehensive)

Comprehensive two-dimensional GC/MS system offering greatly enhanced peak capacity for high-resolution separation of complex pyrolysis product matrices.

Chromatography

High Performance Liquid Chromatography

Separates and quantifies complex liquid-phase mixtures including bio-oil fractions, sugar platform chemicals, and catalytic reaction products.

Chromatography

Ion Chromatography System

Separates and quantifies anions and cations in aqueous process streams, product solutions, and leachate samples with suppressed conductivity detection.

Mass Spectrometry

Shimadzu LC-MS System

Combined liquid chromatography and mass spectrometry for structural identification and quantification of complex bio-product fractions and reaction intermediates.

Shimadzu

Thermal Analysis & Calorimetry

4
Thermal Analysis

Thermogravimetric Analyzer (TGA)

Measures mass change as a function of temperature and time; used for proximate analysis, thermal decomposition kinetics, and combustion/gasification characterisation.

Thermal Analysis

Ash Fusion Tester

Determines fusion and sintering behavior of ash residues under controlled temperature ramps; critical for biomass and coal feedstock characterisation.

Calorimetry

Automatic Bomb Calorimeter

Measures gross calorific value of solid and liquid fuels with automated firing, temperature acquisition, and direct HHV reporting.

Calorimetry

IKA C 200 Semi-Automatic Bomb Calorimeter

Reliable calorific value determination with semi-automated ignition, precise temperature measurement, and direct reporting of higher heating values.

IKA

Composition & Surface Characterization

3
Elemental Analysis

Elemental CHNS Analyzer

Simultaneously quantifies carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, and sulfur content in organic and inorganic samples via combustion and thermal conductivity detection.

Spectroscopy

FTIR-ATR Spectrometer

Identifies functional groups and molecular bonding in solid, liquid, and powder samples using attenuated total reflectance infrared spectroscopy; no sample preparation required.

Surface Analysis

BET Surface Area Analyzer

Determines BET surface area, BJH pore volume, and pore size distribution via multi-point N₂ adsorption–desorption isotherms.
