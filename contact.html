Recruitment

Opportunities to join the CREST Laboratory.

Positions are open on a rolling basis. Write directly to Prof. Vinu with a statement of interest, a CV, and relevant academic transcripts.

The CREST Laboratory actively seeks talented individuals at every career stage, from undergraduate summer interns to experienced postdoctoral researchers. We are committed to rigorous, impactful research in sustainable thermochemical engineering.

Doctoral

Ph.D. scholars

We invite applications from highly motivated candidates with a strong background in chemical engineering, chemistry, or related disciplines. Research areas span thermochemical conversion, catalysis, photocatalysis, and advanced materials for sustainability.

Apply: write to

Postdoctoral

Postdoctoral positions

Opportunities exist under the Institute Postdoctoral Fellowship (IPDF), National Postdoctoral Fellowship (NPDF), and Women Scientist schemes (DST WOS-A/B). Research and Project Associate positions are advertised via the ICSR Job Portal.

  • IITM Institute PDF (IPDF)
  • SERB National PDF (NPDF)
  • DST Women Scientist Schemes (WOS-A / WOS-B)
  • Other national & international fellowships
Apply: write to ICSR Job Portal

Internship

Summer research programme

Undergraduate and Masters students are welcome to apply for short-term summer research internships. Projects are available in thermochemical reactor engineering, catalyst synthesis, computational modelling, and product characterisation. Candidates from IITs, NITs, and other national institutions are encouraged to apply.

Apply: write to

Partnerships

Open to collaborations across domains and geographies.

The CREST Laboratory actively pursues research partnerships with academic groups, industrial organisations, and international consortia that share a commitment to sustainable chemical engineering and thermochemical innovation. We engage in joint project proposals, researcher exchanges, co-supervised doctoral programs, and industry-sponsored research. Prospective collaborators are welcome to reach out with a brief description of the proposed partnership.

Within India

Domestic collaborations

The laboratory is open to academic and industry collaborations within India. We welcome joint research proposals with IITs, NITs, national laboratories (CSIR, DRDO, ISRO), and corporate R&D divisions in the energy, chemicals, and materials sectors.

  • Academic Institutes
  • National Laboratories
  • Industry R&D
  • Government Agencies
Propose a collaboration: write to

Worldwide

International collaborations

We are open to participation in EU Horizon collaborative research projects and other international research partnerships. The laboratory has a track record of productive research exchanges and is well-positioned to contribute to consortia focused on sustainable fuels, circular economy, and advanced reactor engineering.

  • EU Horizon Projects
  • Researcher Exchange
  • Joint PhDs
  • Bilateral Projects
